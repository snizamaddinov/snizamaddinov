About

I'm a backend engineer with a degree in Computer Engineering and a minor in Mathematics. I have been building backend systems since 2021, mostly in Python/Django and PHP/Symfony, and these days much of my work is on LLM and RAG applications. I am happiest when the work keeps me close to the terminal.

I also like running servers myself. I host my own services on a home server, and one of my favourite repositories on GitHub is Awesome-Selfhosted.

Away from the keyboard I run, play football and enjoy filmmaking. I'm a huge fan of The Office and Succession, and I like reading blogs such as Idle Words.
