About

I'm a backend engineer based in Ankara, with a degree in Computer Engineering and a minor in Mathematics. I have been building backend systems since 2021, mostly in Python/Django and PHP/Symfony, and I am happiest when the work keeps me close to the terminal.

For the last few years my focus has been AI-backed backend systems. At BK Mobil I lead Metodbox AI, an assistant that answers from about 65,000 videos and more than 30,000 books using Retrieval-Augmented Generation and LangGraph multi-agent workflows. Right now I am building the next version of an LLM service that generates educational questions and images, and moving it to Kubernetes on AWS.

I also like running servers myself. I keep a home server where I host my own services, and one of my favourite repositories on GitHub is Awesome-Selfhosted, a long list of software you can host yourself.

Away from the keyboard I run, play football and enjoy filmmaking. I'm a huge fan of The Office and Succession, and I could talk about them for hours. I also like reading blogs such as Idle Words.
